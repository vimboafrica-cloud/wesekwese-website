Features

Everything a school office needs, nothing it doesn't

Built specifically for Zimbabwean schools — term or month billing, multiple currencies, and a fee structure that matches how your school actually charges.

Numbered receipts, full audit trail

Every payment gets its own receipt number. A mistake is voided with a reason, never deleted — nothing is ever quietly edited.

A fund for every fee item

Tuition, levy, sports — each is tracked separately, and a fund can never be spent below zero by mistake.

Admin, Bursar and view-only roles

Each person signs in with their own role. Staff who walk away are signed out automatically.

Automatic daily backups

A backup runs every day on its own, with an optional encrypted e-mail copy — and an alert if that e-mail ever fails to send.

Multi-currency, if you need it

USD, ZiG, or both on one receipt — with a currency mode that matches how your school actually takes payment.

Register a whole class in minutes

Download a ready-made spreadsheet per class, hand it to the teacher, then upload it back — nothing is saved until you confirm.

A weekly timetable, built in

Subjects, teachers and time slots in one grid — the same system that runs your fees.

Starting mid-year? No problem

Bring in pupils who already owe fees from before Wese~Kwese existed, and record what they've already paid in bulk.

How it works

Built to work without the internet — because your school office can't always rely on it.

  • 1

    One PC in the office

    Wese~Kwese runs on the school's own computer — no cloud account, no server to rent.

  • 2

    Other PCs, over the network

    Any PC on the same office network opens Wese~Kwese in an ordinary browser — no internet required at all.

  • 3

    Remote access, only if you want it

    A private, encrypted connection (Tailscale) lets you reach it from your own phone when you're away — never an open port on the internet.
